Artist

Sid Sriram

Sid Sriram is one of the most soulful and dynamic playback singers in the Indian music industry. Renowned for his rich, textured voice and unique vocal style, Sid Sriram made his playback singing debut with the song "Adiye" from the film Kadal, composed by A. R. Rahman. Since then, he has delivered numerous chart-topping hits across Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, and Hindi cinema. Fusing Carnatic music with contemporary sounds, he has carved a distinct niche for himself, captivating listeners with his emotive renditions. His ability to blend classical training with modern sensibilities has made him a sought-after singer for both soulful ballads and energetic tracks. Sid Sriram’s expressive singing continues to mesmerize audiences, earning him critical acclaim, prestigious awards, and a growing fanbase across generations.
